Who we are

We are 10men

A Prague-based vocal group that has been captivating audiences since 2015, both in the Czech vocal scene and beyond.

Our name refers to the number of founding members, while the number of active members has evolved over time. We offer a wide-ranging repertoire from pop hits and jazz to swing, and on special occasions, we also perform classical music.

We perform at concerts, festivals, and private events.

LAMEN project

Do you know our LAMEN project?
